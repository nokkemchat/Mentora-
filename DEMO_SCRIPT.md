# Mentora — Demo Recording Script (~3–4 min)

**How to record:** Press **⌘⇧5** → choose "Record Entire Screen" or "Record Selected Portion"
(drag a box around the browser window) → **Record**. Do the takes below in order.
Stop the recording with the stop button in the menu bar. Tip: close extra tabs first,
and set the browser to full screen for a clean shot.

**Before recording — prep checklist (2 min):**
- [ ] Dashboard open at `http://localhost:5173` — logged in, on the **Past Papers** page
- [ ] A paper with **all students marked** (so the viewer is full of annotations)
- [ ] Downloaded PDF ready in the Downloads folder (for take 7)
- [ ] Browser zoom ~110%, dark theme active

---

## TAKE 1 — The library (0:00–0:30)
**On screen:** Papers page, scroll slowly through the paper cards.

**Say:**
> "This is Mentora — an AI marking and tutoring platform for Cambridge and ZIMSEC
> past papers. Every paper here is fully ingested: questions, mark schemes, and
> page images are extracted automatically, and students' handwritten submissions
> are marked by an AI examiner."

## TAKE 2 — Theme switch (0:30–0:45)
**On screen:** Click the **sun/moon toggle** in the sidebar (top). Let the light theme
settle for a second, then switch back to dark.

**Say:**
> "The whole console is dual-theme — dark for long marking sessions, light for
> daylight — one click, fully consistent across every screen."

## TAKE 3 — The marking workspace (0:45–1:30)
**On screen:** Open a marked paper → the **Annotations** view. Slowly page through
**Page 1 → 6** using the page switcher. Hover one of the red margin badges
(✓ 2/2) to show the tooltip.

**Say:**
> "Here's a student's script, marked. Every tick, mark code, and correction is
> placed by the AI against the official mark scheme — point by point, exactly
> where the student wrote. The margin badges show each subpart's score, and at
> the bottom right you can see the total for each question on the page."

## TAKE 4 — The examiner's reasoning (1:30–2:15)
**On screen:** Right panel — **Question Marking Explanations**. Scroll through one
question's subparts: show a "Mark Scheme Points Earned" card and a "Lost Marks"
card with the red-ink correction.

**Say:**
> "Every mark is explainable. The panel shows the exact mark-scheme points earned
> and lost per subpart, with a teacher-style correction showing how the answer
> should have been done — so students learn, not just get scored."

## TAKE 5 — AI-painted paper (2:15–2:45)
**On screen:** Toggle **AI Photo** on one page (the photorealistic red-ink version),
then back to **Original** to show the untouched scan.

**Say:**
> "Mentora can also repaint the script photorealistically with authentic red
> teacher ink — or show the original scan — so the student sees their own paper,
> marked."

## TAKE 6 — Booklet totals (2:45–3:00)
**On screen:** Jump to **Page 1** — point at the **Booklet Total** stamp in the
top-left corner.

**Say:**
> "The booklet total is stamped on the first page, and every question's total on
> the page where the work appears — the same way a human examiner marks."

## TAKE 7 — Download the marked paper (3:00–3:30)
**On screen:** Click **⬇ Download PDF** → show the downloaded PDF opening
(scrolled through 2–3 pages quickly).

**Say:**
> "One click downloads the fully marked paper as a PDF — every annotation
> included — ready to print or send to the student."

## TAKE 8 — Closing line (3:30–3:45)
**On screen:** Back on the Papers page, pause.

**Say:**
> "Mentora: mark a full class in minutes — consistently, explainably, and in the
> student's own handwriting."

---

**Recording tips:**
- Move the mouse slowly; pause 1s on anything you mention.
- If a take goes wrong, keep talking and redo the click — you can cut it later.
- Music (optional): add a quiet lo-fi track at 10% volume in your editor.
